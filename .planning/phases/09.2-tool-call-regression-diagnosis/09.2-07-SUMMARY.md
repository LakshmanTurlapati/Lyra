Post-downsample train split: **7,519** samples — 4,909 tool-calling (65.3%), 1,306 knowledge (17.4%), 1,304 code (17.3%).

The top-5 prefixes after downsample are now dominated by "no-tool" patterns ("I can help with that without needing to look anything up.", "Based on what I know, I can provide a direct answer...") — legitimate training signals for when the model should *not* call a tool — rather than the canned post-tool-call summaries that drove the H1 regression.

### Validator Relaxation + Regression Guards

The existing `scripts/validate_format.py` rejected single-turn conversations ending in a tool_call (required exactly N tool-result turns after any assistant with tool_calls). This was a silent structural cause of `pct_end_in_tool_call == 0` in the 09.1 corpus — the generation pipeline couldn't emit them even if it wanted to.

Updated Rule 5 to allow the final assistant with tool_calls to have zero subsequent tool-result turns (single-turn "emit tool_call and stop" pattern). Mid-conversation tool_calls still require matching tool-result turns, guarded by a new regression test (`test_mid_conversation_tool_call_still_needs_result`).
